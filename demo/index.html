Read on sea glass. Touch jelly. See through the tide.

A premium interface material for any brand. Frosted, softly tinted sea glass holds everything you read. Squishy jelly dents under your finger and peels back when you let go. A liquid tide at the edges keeps content faintly visible under the navigation.

Four depths

One tint, four depths.

Every surface takes its color from the brand tint mixed with white. Deeper means more important. Deep is kept for the one main action on a screen.

Thickness

Float is glass. Read is solid.

Thickness is opacity and blur together, chosen by the job, never by taste. Text on floating glass must pass 4.5:1 against the darkest thing that can scroll behind it. If it fails, go one step thicker.

tideglass
Thin56% · 12px blur
Regular68% · 20px blur. Bars, tab bar, composer.
Thick84% · 28px blur. Menus, sheets, popovers.
Solid100% · no blur. Anything you read.
Touch

Dent on touch. Peel on release.

Press into soft jelly and it gives a little. Lift your finger and it clings for a beat, pulls up toward you, then settles. One rebound, never a wobble. Size picks the tier; a role can only make it calmer.

S · up to 56px

Icon buttons, orbs, avatars, switches. The most squish.

dent 1.035 × 0.93, sink 1px · peel 0.985 × 1.05, lift −1.5px

M · 57–160px

Chips, pills, tabs, tiles, segments. S and M lean with your finger.

dent 1.02 × 0.955, sink 0.5px · peel 0.992 × 1.03, lift −1px

L · wider

Rows and cards barely move. Long-press charges, then pops.

dent 0.985 even · peel 1.004 · hold 350ms

Firm

Money and loss. No tint, no peel, no wobble.

dent 0.97 even · eases back in 360ms, no overshoot

Lift

Small keys your finger covers grow and rise so you can see them.

grows to 1.15, rises 4px · glides back

None

Navigation

Jelly you can drag. Tide you can see through.

Tabs and segments share one selection blob. It glides to your choice and stretches on the way. Press and slide along a tab bar and the blob follows your finger like a drop of jelly.

Tap a tab, or press and slide across the bar. Arrow keys work too.
