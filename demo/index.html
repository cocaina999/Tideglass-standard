Tideglass

Read on sea glass. Touch jelly. See through the tide.

A design standard for app interfaces, in any brand color. Text sits on frosted, tinted glass. Buttons are soft jelly: they dent when you press and spring back when you let go. At the top and bottom of the screen, a clear tide lets content show through the bars.

Four depths

One tint, four depths.

Every surface is the brand tint mixed with white. The deeper the color, the more important the element. Deep is saved for the one main action on a screen.

Thickness

Float is glass. Read is solid.

Thickness sets opacity and blur together, and the job decides it. Text on floating glass must pass 4.5:1 against the darkest thing that can scroll behind it. If it fails, use the next thickness up.

tideglass
Thin56% · 12px blur
Regular68% · 20px blur. Bars, tab bar, composer.
Thick84% · 28px blur. Menus, sheets, popovers.
Solid100% · no blur. Anything you read.
Touch

Dent on touch. Peel on release.

Press a control and it gives a little. Let go and it holds for a moment, lifts toward you, then settles with one small rebound. Size decides how much it moves. A role can make it move less, never more.

S · up to 56px

Icon buttons, orbs, avatars, switches. The most squish.

dent 1.035 × 0.93, sink 1px · peel 0.985 × 1.05, lift −1.5px

M · 57–160px

Chips, pills, tabs, tiles, segments. S and M lean with your finger.

dent 1.02 × 0.955, sink 0.5px · peel 0.992 × 1.03, lift −1px

L · wider

Rows and cards barely move. Long-press charges, then pops.

dent 0.985 even · peel 1.004 · hold 350ms

Firm

For payments, deletes and locks. No tint and no bounce.

dent 0.97 even · eases back in 360ms, no overshoot

Lift

Keys small enough to hide under your finger grow and rise, so you can still see them.

grows to 1.15, rises 4px · glides back

None

Links dim. Typing surfaces and disabled controls stay still.

Navigation

One blob marks the selection.

Tab bars and segmented controls use the same blob. Tap and it glides over, stretching a little on the way. Press and slide along the tab bar and it follows your finger.

Tap a tab, or press and slide across the bar. Arrow keys work too.
